For small businesses and for teams inside larger companies. I scope each one together, so you know what you're getting before we begin.

1. AI readiness check

2. One workflow, built

We pick a single repetitive process, such as reconciling invoices, reviewing contracts or tracking renewals, and I build and test an AI-assisted version of it with your team.

3. Team enablement

Hands-on training and short video lessons in the style of “AI in 5”, so your people use the tools confidently and keep using them.
